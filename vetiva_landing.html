Who is Dangote Petroleum Refinery & Petrochemicals FZE?+

The company behind Africa’s largest and most advanced refinery, located in the Lekki Free Zone, Lagos. Full details are in the offer prospectus.

How do I subscribe to the offer?+

Open a VETIVA-INVEST account now. When the offer is available, choose your number of shares, pay securely and receive confirmation in the app.

Do I need a CSCS account?+

Yes. One is created for you when you open your account, and your shares are held in your name at the CSCS.

What is the minimum number of shares I can buy?+

10 shares, which is ₦5,250.00 at ₦525.00 per share.

How will I receive my shares?+

Allotted shares are credited to your CSCS account. You can track them in the VETIVA-INVEST app.

Can I apply through the VETIVA-INVEST app?+

Yes. The app is available on Google Play and the App Store, and you can also use client.vetiva.com in your browser.

Is this a good investment?+

Every investment carries risk. Read the offer prospectus carefully and consider your own circumstances before you invest.

Where can I find more information?+

Contact clientsupport@vetiva.com or call 0201-889-1816, and read the offer prospectus for full details.
