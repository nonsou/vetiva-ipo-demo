No. A BVN is a Central Bank identity number — like your fingerprint, not your key. Moving money requires your card, PIN, OTP, or banking password, none of which we ever ask for. Nobody, including Vetiva, can debit your account with a BVN alone.

Shares in Nigeria are held at the CSCS in the owner's name, and the SEC and CSCS require a BVN to open that account. It protects you: it's how the system guarantees the shares belong to you and not someone with a similar name. Every licensed broker has the same requirement.

One thing: verify that you are you, and open your CSCS account. It's encrypted in transit and at rest, handled under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, never sold, and never shared for marketing.
